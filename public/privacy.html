Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

SnapDraft doesn't need an account. Your plans stay on your device unless you choose to share them.

SnapDraft is made by Hummingbird Tech, LLC ("we"). This policy covers the SnapDraft app for iPhone and iPad, the SnapDraft web app at snapdraftapp.com/app, and this website.

iPhone and iPad app

The app does not collect any data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to any server of ours.

  • Plans are saved as .snapdraft files on your device, in Files under On My iPhone or On My iPad › SnapDraft, or wherever you choose to keep them. If you keep them in a cloud service such as iCloud Drive, that service's own terms apply.
  • Settings such as your measurement units and Apple Pencil preference are stored on your device.
  • Opening the About links or emailing us leaves the app and goes to your browser or mail app.

Web app and website

  • The web app saves plans in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not uploaded to us.
  • The web app uses Google Analytics to count visits and understand how SnapDraft is used. Google receives the page address, your browser and device type and an approximate location derived from your IP address. It is configured without analytics cookies. See Google's privacy policy.
  • The site is hosted by Netlify, which processes standard request information (such as IP address, page address and browser) to deliver pages and keep the service secure.

Shared links

A SnapDraft share link or QR code contains the plan itself. Anyone with the link can open a copy. We don't store shared plans, but when a link is opened in the web app, its address, including the plan, reaches our host and may be recorded as the page address in analytics. Only share links to plans you're comfortable sending this way.

Email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply to you.

Children

SnapDraft is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Delete plans in the app or in Files, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • You can block analytics with your browser's privacy settings or a content blocker; the web app works without it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
