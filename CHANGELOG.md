# Changelog

What's changed in the SnapDraft web app at [snapdraftapp.com](https://snapdraftapp.com/app). You can also read this in the app under Help › Updates.

New entries are added automatically when a pull request merges. See [how the changelog works](CONTRIBUTING.md#changelog).

## 2026.11.03 ([#19](https://github.com/RohitRajendran/snapdraft/pull/19))

### New

- Drag a corner to move every wall joined there at once, in one undo step.

### Improved

- A selected wall's handles move just that wall's end, so it can pull away from a corner or snap back onto one.
- Boxes hide the grid inside them, without covering walls or other boxes.
- Box labels are fitted to their boxes: centered, kept upright, and shortened or turned when they don't fit.
- A new SnapDraft icon.
- Added Privacy and Support pages.

### Fixed

- A click right after placing an opening, switching tools or dragging could select the wall behind a door or window.

## 2026.10.14 ([#16](https://github.com/RohitRajendran/snapdraft/pull/16))

### New

- Color-code boxes with five preset colors or a custom color. New boxes rotate through the presets.

## 2026.07.07 ([#14](https://github.com/RohitRajendran/snapdraft/pull/14))

### Improved

- A box's sizes are now called Width and Length, rather than Width and Height.

## 2026.07.04 ([#13](https://github.com/RohitRajendran/snapdraft/pull/13))

### Fixed

- The home page now loads properly for people who have opened the app before.

## 2026.05.05 ([#10](https://github.com/RohitRajendran/snapdraft/pull/10))

### New

- Place doors and windows on walls, then drag them along or across the wall. Doors can change swing side and hinge end.

## 2026.04.29

### New

- Hand tool for panning without touching the plan. You can also hold Space and drag.

### Improved

- A clearer layout for the help window.

## 2026.04.28

### Improved

- Dimension fields accept millimeters.

## 2026.04.26

### New

- Switch between imperial and metric units in Preferences.

## 2026.04.25

### New

- A sample bedroom plan for first-time visitors.

## 2026.03.25 ([#8](https://github.com/RohitRajendran/snapdraft/pull/8))

### New

- Twist two fingers to rotate a selected box on a touch screen.
- On phones, tapping an item shows a compact bar with Undo, Redo, Edit and Delete.

## 2026.03.22 ([#6](https://github.com/RohitRajendran/snapdraft/pull/6))

### Fixed

- Two-finger pinch zoom and pan now work on touch screens.

## 2026.03.20 ([#5](https://github.com/RohitRajendran/snapdraft/pull/5))

- A tape measure visual for the Measure tool.

## 2026.03.19

### New
